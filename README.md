# PayneLab - Movement & Control Guide

Welcome to the **PayneLab** movement system! This guide explains how to control your character, dive, ragdoll, and recover with style.

---

## 🎮 Basic Controls

| Action | Input | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Move** | Left Stick (LS) | Walk or run in any direction. |
| **Look** | Right Stick (RS) | Rotate your camera/head view. |
| **Jump** | A Button | Jump into the air. Hold for higher jumps. |
| **Sprint** | Left Trigger (LT) | Run faster while moving. |

---

## 🤸 Special Modes

### 1. Dive Mode (Superman Style)
**How to Activate:** Click **Right Stick (RS)** while in the air or standing.

*   **What it does:** Your character leaps forward into a "Superman" dive pose.
*   **Legs:** Extend straight back naturally. The faster you dive, the straighter they get. If you spin or flip, knees tuck automatically.
*   **Arms & Head:** You have **full control**. 
    *   **Right Stick (RS):** Steer left/right, tilt torso forward/back, and look around.
    *   **Left Stick (LS):** Perform acrobatic flips and rolls!

#### Dive Controls Breakdown
| Input | Action | Visual Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RS Left / Right** | **Turn / Yaw** | Rotates your whole body left or right mid-air (moderate strength for smooth steering). |
| **RS Up / Down** | **Torso Tilt** | Subtly leans your upper body forward (nose dive) or backward (slight brake). |
| **RS (Hold)** | **Look Around** | Your head tracks the stick so you can see where you're going. |
| **LS Up** | **Front Flip** | Strong pitch rotation - tucks legs and initiates a forward somersault. |
| **LS Down** | **Back Flip** | Strong pitch rotation - tucks legs and initiates a backward somersault. |
| **LS Left / Right** | **Barrel Roll** | Full-strength roll around your spine axis for corkscrew spins (works in air AND on ground!). |
| **Release RS** (on ground) | **Stand Up** | Performs a cool "push-up" animation: legs tuck under, feet plant, then extend to stand. |

> **💡 Pro Tip:** Combine **LS Flips** with **RS Steering** for complex aerial corkscrews! Hold LS Up + RS Left for a twisting front flip. The legs will dynamically extend when you straighten out and tuck tight when you spin. Barrel rolls work continuously in both aerial and grounded states!

### 2. Ragdoll Mode (Physics Fun)
**How to Activate:** Click **Right Stick (RS) + Left Stick (LS)** at the same time.

*   **What it does:** Your character goes limp and reacts purely to physics (gravity, impacts, explosions).
*   **Legs:** **Stiff but floppy.** They won't turn into spaghetti; they keep some shape but still flail realistically when hit.
*   **Arms & Head:** You have **full control**!
    *   Even while ragdolling, you can pose your arms by looking/moving the stick. This prevents them from looking dead while letting them still react to hits.
    *   Look around freely! This is great if you use camera mods like "RagdollView" to see from your character's eyes. It helps reduce dizziness by letting you control the view.
*   **How to Stand Up:** While on the ground, click **RS** (just the Right Stick). Your character will do a physical get-up animation, planting feet and pushing off the ground.

## 🔄 Mode Switching Cheat Sheet

| Current State | Input | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standing / Running** | Click **RS** | Enter **Dive Mode** (if jumping) or prepare to dive. |
| **Standing / Running** | Click **RS + LS** | Toggle **Ragdoll Mode** ON/OFF. |
| **Diving** | Click **RS** (on ground) | **Stand Up** (animated recovery). |
| **Ragdolling** | Click **RS** (on ground) | **Stand Up** (animated recovery). |
| **Ragdolling** | Click **RS + LS** | Turn **Ragdoll Mode OFF** (return to normal standing). |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

*   **The "Superman" Feel:** In Dive mode, try looking down and holding sprint. Your legs will extend fully for maximum speed and style.
*   **Safe Landing:** If you are diving fast and need to stop spinning, release the look stick. Your legs will automatically extend to stabilize you.
*   **Ragdoll Posing:** Don't let your arms hang dead in Ragdoll mode! Use the look stick to hold your arms out or cover your face during an explosion for dramatic effect.
*   **Recovery:** The stand-up animation is physical. If you are on a slope or ledge, the character will try to plant their feet under their center of gravity before standing.

---

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

*   **"My legs look weird in ragdoll!"**
    *   Make sure you aren't accidentally in Dive mode. Ragdoll legs are stiffer by design to prevent "spaghetti" flopping.
*   **"I can't get up!"**
    *   Ensure you are touching the ground. You cannot stand up while mid-air. Press **RS** firmly once.
*   **"The camera is dizzying in ragdoll."**
    *   Try gently moving the Right Stick to re-orient your head upright. You have full head control even when your body is limp!

Enjoy experimenting with the physics! 🚀

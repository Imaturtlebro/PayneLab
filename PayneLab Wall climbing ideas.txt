That is a textbook parkour and rock-climbing technique called "Wall Smearing" (or Flagging)—and in VR, it looks and feels unbelievable.
In real life, when you hang from a ledge sideways or hold on with one hand, you never let both legs dangle like wet noodles. You instinctively plant your inside foot against the wall to create friction, stop your body from swinging like a pendulum, and give yourself a platform to push off from.
Here is why adding this to PayneLab is a massive upgrade for both visuals and combat:
1. The "Uncharted / John Wick" Ledge Hang Shot
Imagine this scenario in slow-mo:
You run along a catwalk, dive off the edge, and catch a steel pipe with your left hand.
In vanilla BONELAB, your lower body just swings around blindly.
With PayneLab Wall-Planting: Your left hip rotates, your left knee flexes, and your boot firmly plants against the concrete wall, stabilizing your torso.
With your body locked in place, you draw a pistol with your free right hand, lean out sideways, and pick off enemies below you while hanging one-handed.

4. Ledge-Push & Corner Mantles
It also sets up an offensive maneuver: The Ledge Kick-Off.
While hanging and braced against the wall with your foot, tapping Jump (Right A) or pushing away from the wall can trigger an impulse from that planted foot, launching you laterally around the corner or vaulting you over the railing.
